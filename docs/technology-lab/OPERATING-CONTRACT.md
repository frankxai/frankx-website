# FrankX Technology Lab — Phase 1 Operating Contract
Date: 2026-11-08. Status: proposed / review required.

## Positioning
FrankX is the independent editorial, commercial and customer-facing lab. Starlight Intelligence supplies reusable agent runtime, evidence and integrations. GenCreator supports production and distribution. No new cloud resources or separate brand domains required.

## Commercial hierarchy
1. Architecture Review: discovery, existing-state audit, prioritized plan. Indicative €750–€1,500; quote after scope.
2. Integration Sprint: fixed deliverable and acceptance criteria. Indicative €2,500–€7,500.
3. Ongoing Technology Partnership: support, evals and improvement. Indicative €500–€2,000/month.
4. Affiliate commerce and sponsored work: secondary; track actual attributed margin and explicit disclosures.
5. Software subscriptions: only after a repeated customer workflow has demonstrated retention.

Prices are hypotheses, not published commitments. Quote net of VAT where applicable. Track delivery hours, gross margin, payment terms, and scope changes.

## Agent pipeline
Scout → evidence intake → lab evaluation plan → human-approved test → content draft → claim/rights/disclosure gate → publication approval → attributed conversion → monthly unit-economics review.
Every record requires: canonical ID, owner, source URL, claim status (verified / vendor stated / research / hypothesis), last checked, relationship status, disclosure, cost, outcome and next action.
Do not auto-send outreach, auto-buy hardware, fabricate test results, or claim manufacturer endorsement. Respect API and platform terms.

## Review methodology
For hardware, record model, firmware, OS, test date, power mode, workload, repetitions, ambient conditions, variance and limitations. Label access: owned, loaned, sponsored, or desk research. Record return obligations. Disclose referral links and sponsorship near relevant recommendations. Keep sponsor approval out of editorial verdicts.

## Launch gates
- Verify actual Vercel repository and domain mapping before deployment.
- Confirm Next.js build and mobile accessibility in CI / preview.
- Review existing homepage claims before expanding traffic.
- Publish first genuine benchmark and integration demo only after reproducing it.
- Validate lead capture and privacy policy before collecting form data; initial contact is mailto.
- Confirm commercial terms and partner rights before any outreach.

## 30-day scorecard
Qualified inquiries, discovery calls, paid projects, contribution margin, published verified tests, content-to-lead conversion, affiliate earnings, hardware access agreements and infrastructure spend. Zero is a valid baseline; never invent performance.
